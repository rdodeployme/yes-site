What the establishment fee covers

The establishment fee is normally $5,000 to $15,000, depending on the number of programs, sites and streams. It covers scoping, the stream list, the baseline period, the method sheet and onboarding: the four weeks that get a customer from first call to live dashboard.

Foundation Members who join in the first 12 months pay none of it. The monthly fee applies from the switch-on date.

What Foundation Members receive

  • Establishment fee waived. Scoping, baseline, method sheet and onboarding at no charge.
  • Pricing held for the first term. The monthly fee you join at is the fee for the term.
  • Input into the method roadmap. Foundation Members are consulted before a method change is published.
  • Named as a founding customer. Optional, in the Yarta Method change log.

What is included

Reporting is included. Collection is not.

Yarta is the reporting layer. The physical work of collecting and processing material is a separate contract.

Included in the monthly fee

  • Yarta Dashboard. Live, for the team that owns the reporting.
  • Data entry and verification. Every stream entered and verified by Yarta staff.
  • Monthly gap email. Per council, or per site for business.
  • Yarta Report. Per program, site or period, with method version and report ID.
  • Exports. CSV of the ledger, the Yarta Report as a PDF, and a Scope 3 Category 5 table for business.
  • Method pack and version history. The factor library, sources and change log.
  • Named Yarta data operator. One person who enters your data and answers for it.
  • Onboarding. Switched on in 30 days, at no charge for Foundation Members.

Not included

  • Physical collection and processing. Contracted separately, with Recycle Group or your existing contractors. Yarta reports either.
  • Offsets or credits. Yarta reports avoided emissions under a stated method. It does not issue offsets or credits.
  • Assurance. Yarta is designed to support third-party assurance with evidence, method and exports. It does not provide the assurance itself.

Pricing questions

Before you sign.

What finance teams ask before they sign.

Is there a contract term?

Yes. Yarta is an annual subscription, priced by the month. We set the terms out in writing before you commit to anything.

What happens after 12 months for Foundation Members?

The monthly fee continues as before. No establishment fee is ever charged to a Foundation Member, in year two or later.

Can we start with one program?

Yes. Start with a single hard waste program or one transfer station, then add kerbside and whole-of-council entries once the first report has been through a council meeting. The monthly fee does not change when programs are added.

Do you charge per report?

No. Reports and exports are included in the subscription. There is no charge per program or per report.
