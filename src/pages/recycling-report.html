07 · Method and evidenceYarta Method v1.0 draft

A · Category 5 inventory. GHG Protocol waste-type-specific method: processing (0.05 t CO₂-e per t handled) + transport (120 km × 0.15 kg per t-km), both illustrative until metered, + landfilled tonnes × NGA landfill factors. C&I factor on residual until a composition audit.

B · Avoided emissions. Steel recovered × 0.44, plus other recycled tonnes × the stream's factor (NSW DECCW 2010, Table 4; flagged 2010). Reported separately, never deducted from A, not an offset.

Recycled. Baled or sorted material delivered to a licensed reprocessor, with a docket. Mass. Weighbridge tonnes; counts at published unit weights (mattress 80 kg, tyre 9.5 kg, appliance 60 kg, vehicle 1,200 kg).

AWeighbridge docket or processor certificate. Counts in full.
BSigned count sheet or contractor report. Counts, flagged.
CCustomer statement without documentation. Recorded; excluded from the Yarta Score until evidenced.

Scope. Material handed over to Recycle Group under this program, plus whole-of-organisation entries evidenced by the council and graded as above, with no processing or transport attributed to them. Stored material is reclassified in the period it moves. Issued by Recycle Group as the processing operator; not independently assured.
