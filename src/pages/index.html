04

Verification

Only YES enters the data. That is why the number holds.

If customers typed in their own figures, no report built on them would be credible. So they do not. YES staff enter every handover, check it against the docket or weighbridge record, and file it in one of three buckets.

THE MONTHLY EMAIL

Fill in the gaps.

Once a month YES sends an automated email listing every stream we know moved and every one we cannot yet account for. You tell us where it went. We enter it and mark it as verified or as customer-evidenced.

THREE BUCKETS

Every tonne lands in one place.

Landfilled counts against you. Recycled counts for you: delivered to a licensed reprocessor, with a docket. Stored is neutral, parked until it moves. Nothing is averaged and nothing is netted off.

THE YES METHOD

One documented method.

Every factor has a source, a status and a version. The Category 5 inventory uses the government's NGA landfill factors. Avoided emissions use NSW DECCW 2010 factors, flagged for refresh and reported separately. Assumptions are labelled as assumptions until measured data replaces them.
