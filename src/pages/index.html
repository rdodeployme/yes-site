The YARTA story

Four elements. One purpose: shaping Country for tomorrow.

YARTA is built around a simple belief: the future of Country is shaped by what we do today. Our logo brings together four ideas, Country, water, sun and tomorrow, as one connected symbol.

Country

At the centre of YARTA is Country: living, connected and constantly changing. The decisions we make about resources, waste and materials become part of that story. YARTA exists to help organisations understand those decisions, and make better ones.

Water

Sun

Tomorrow

03

Understand

Ten categories. One score.

The Yindyamarra Score is the average of the categories you report. Each one is scored 0 to 100 against the eco standard or your own target, from the figures behind it. A category you don't report is left out, not scored zero.

Energy & Renewables

Electricity, gas, solar and batteries. Renewable share and use against your baseline.

Fleet & Transport

Fuel, kilometres, electrification, charging and flights.

Carbon & Emissions

Scope 1, 2 and reported Scope 3 with NGA factors. Avoided emissions shown separately, never netted.

Water

Potable use per bed, student, site or resident, and the share from recycled, rain and stormwater.

Waste & Resource Recovery

Every stream, from kerbside to clinical: recovered, landfilled or treated. Diversion and contamination.

Circular Economy

Recycled-content procurement, reuse, packaging and take-back programs.

Land & Soil

Land rehabilitated, contaminated land remediated, soil reused.

Biodiversity & Nature

Trees planted and native vegetation, habitat and wetland restored or protected.

Community & Participation

Programs, events and the people who take part.

Governance & Improvement

Data completeness, evidence behind each figure, complaints closed, projects delivered.

06

Your data

Your data in. Your results out. Then the roadmap.

You send what you already hold. Yarta enters it, grades the evidence behind each figure and calculates the score, the figures and the two emissions figures with one documented method. Then it shows where your targets would put you and what to do next.

THE MONTHLY EMAIL

Send what you have.

Once a month Yarta emails a list of what it needs for the period: waste and recycling reports, utility bills, fuel and fleet records, invoices. Reply with the documents. Yarta enters the figures and grades the evidence behind each one.

EVIDENCE GRADES

Every figure carries its record.

A bill, a docket, a certificate or a contractor report is Grade A. A statement or an extract is Grade B. An estimate is Grade C and says so. Nothing is averaged and nothing is netted off.

THE Yarta METHOD

One documented method.

Every factor has a source, a status and a version. The Category 5 inventory uses the government's NGA landfill factors. Avoided emissions use NSW DECCW 2010 factors, flagged for refresh and reported separately. Assumptions are labelled as assumptions until measured data replaces them.
