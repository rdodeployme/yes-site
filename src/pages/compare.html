02 · Where Yarta is the better fit

When the number has to hold up, and has to change.

You need one number across many suppliers

A contractor's portal reports its own contract. Yarta reports the whole organisation, the same way for every supplier, so the score is comparable year on year and against the eco standards.

You are asked "what is behind that figure?"

A council meeting, a tender panel or an assurance provider asks. Every Yarta figure points to a document and a grade, and the method is published.

You want to know what to do, not only where you are

The roadmap turns the gap between your score and the eco standards into suggestions with an estimated effect, and introduces providers who hold the right accreditation.

03 · Where something else is the better fit

Honestly.

You have a data team and only need an emissions inventory

Carbon software you run yourself may be enough. Yarta's value is in the ten categories, the benchmarks and the roadmap, and in a data operator doing the entry.

You need financial-statement assurance

Yarta does not provide assurance. It produces the evidence, the method and the export an assurance provider will ask for. You still need the provider.

You need bespoke strategy or an engagement

A consultant can build a decarbonisation strategy for your circumstances. Yarta measures, benchmarks and suggests; it does not write your strategy.

You only need your contractor's tonnage

Then the contractor's portal is enough. Yarta can use its data as one input among many, graded for what it is.

They also work together. Yarta can use your contractor's report as evidence, and its exports feed your carbon software or your assurance provider.

04 · Five questions for any provider

Ask us the same ones.

  1. Who enters the data? If it is you, what does that cost your team each month?
  2. Can you show me the document behind this number? Every figure, not a sample.
  3. What factor and which edition produced this emissions figure? And can it be reproduced next year?
  4. How do you benchmark me, and with whose data? If the answer is other customers, ask whether mine will be shared too.
  5. What do I do next? And who will do it, with what accreditation?
