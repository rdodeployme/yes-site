01

The product family

One report. Six ways to read it.

Every product below is a view of the same verified ledger. Nothing is calculated twice and nothing is calculated differently.

  1. 01

    YES Report

    The report you receive for each project or period

    Contains the period, the streams, tonnes by fate with the three-bucket split for every stream, steel recovered, landfill avoided, the self-declared YES Score, the roadmap, both emissions figures, the method version, the issuer and a report ID. The five products below are its parts.

    Sample report →
  2. 02

    YES Dashboard

    Live reporting portal

    Contains the score tiles, recovered tonnes by stream, the roadmap and recommended help, the ledger of every handover, the monthly gap summary and both emissions figures, updated the day material moves.

  3. 03

    YES Data

    Underlying recovery and recycling data

    Contains every ledger entry with its date, quantity, bucket, evidence grade and evidence reference, exportable as CSV.

  4. 04

    YES Circularity

    Material recovery and circular-economy outcomes

    Contains the material recovery rate, tonnes delivered to licensed reprocessors and landfill volume avoided, by stream and in total.

  5. 05

    YES Carbon

    Emissions · two figures, never netted

    Contains the Scope 3 Category 5 inventory figure (processing, transport and landfilled tonnes at NGA factors) and, separately, the avoided-emissions estimate per stream, each with its factor and source. Streams with no avoided-emissions factor are marked pending.

  6. 06

    YES Benchmark

    Comparison against previous periods or sites

    Contains period-on-period and site-on-site movement in every tile, and which streams moved the number.

One number a board can read. Three parts an auditor can check.

05

Exports and integrations

Built to leave the dashboard.

The figures are yours. Every export carries the evidence reference and the method citation, so the number in your annual report is the number on the ledger.

  • CSV of every ledger entry. Date, stream, quantity, bucket, evidence grade, evidence reference and the operator's initials.
  • PDF report. The YES Report for the period, print-ready.
  • Scope 3 export. Waste generated in operations, mapped to GHG Protocol Scope 3 Category 5: mass, treatment, factor, NGA edition and method for every line. Avoided emissions travel in a separate table.
  • Annual report figures. Tonnes by fate, steel, landfill avoided and both emissions figures as a citable table, each with its YES Method reference and the wording to use.
  • API access. Read access to your ledger and tiles for your own systems. Roadmap

Evidence-based, not marketing-based. No trees saved. Every equivalence shown with its source.

The Category 5 figure uses the government's NGA landfill factors with supplier-specific processing data. Avoided emissions use NSW DECCW 2010 factors, flagged for refresh, and are reported separately: never deducted, never an offset. Streams with no avoided-emissions factor are reported as recovered tonnes with no estimate, and are marked as pending on the report.

06

The difference

A tonnage line is not a report.

Most organisations already receive a number from their contractor. The question is what it can support.

A contractor's tonnage report

  • One figure per invoice, entered by the contractor
  • A diversion percentage with no method behind it
  • No evidence reference a reader can follow
  • No carbon figure, or one without a source
  • A different format from every contractor
  • Nothing that survives an auditor's question

The YES Report

  • Every handover entered and verified by YES
  • Three buckets, and Stored never counts as recycled
  • An evidence reference and operator's initials on every entry
  • An inventory figure and an avoided figure, never netted, each with its source
  • One method across every stream and every contractor
  • A report ID and a method version that can be reproduced
