The proposed names, and where each one stands.

Yarta wants its products carried by First Nations words for what they measure: the score, the report, land, rain, trees, today and what happens next. A word goes on the public site only after the custodians of its language confirm the word, its meaning and this use, in writing. Until then the public site keeps the English labels and the names show in preview only.

02 · Getting it right

How a word gets onto the site.

1 · One language, or say which

The list mixes Gamilaraay/Yuwaalaraay, Wiradjuri and Kaurna. Each is a different nation's language. Either choose one language for the product, or say plainly on the site which word comes from which language and why.

2 · Record the source

Who gave Yarta each word, when, and in what context. A word from an Elder or a language centre is confirmed with them. A word taken from a website or a list is not a source.

3 · Ask the custodians, and pay for their time

Gamilaraay/Yuwaalaraay and Wiradjuri words go to the relevant language centre or Aboriginal Language Advisory group; Kaurna names go through Kaurna Warra Karrpanthi. Ask for the spelling, the meaning, whether the word may be used commercially, and how they want to be credited.

4 · Written permission on file

Permission is written, names the words and the use (a product name on a public website and in reports), and is kept with the trade mark file. Yindyamarra's permission is held for the Yindyamarra draft; it needs to say it covers this site too.

5 · Credit on the site

Each word is credited on this page and in the footer in the form the custodians choose, with the language named. No word is presented as a Yarta invention.

6 · No imitation art

Words are the language of a nation, not a style. The site uses no dot-painting or other Aboriginal art styles, symbols or AI-generated imagery. The logo is to be commissioned from an Aboriginal artist, and any Country photography supplied and approved by Traditional Owners.

03 · Where the names would sit

The product, in these words.

Yawu ReportThe whole report: the summary page, the figures behind it and the roadmap. Gamilaraay/Yuwaalaraay: yes.
Yindyamarra ScoreThe overall score, and the way of working behind it. Wiradjuri: respect, taking responsibility.
Yarta · what happens nextEnvironmental sustainability as a whole, and the roadmap: the score at the targets, the estimate with Yarta help, the plan by quarter. Kaurna: land, country, earth.

Yurra, Yuuruu, Yamarr, Yuwalla, YurrandaaliRenewable energy, rain and stormwater, waterways, trees, and the planting program: the environmental modules planned beyond recycling. Two of the five check out so far (Yuuruu, Yurrandaali).
