01

Why YES exists

The material was always measured. The customer never saw the number.

Councils and businesses hand over problem streams. Recycle Group recycles them. YES turns that into figures a board, a councillor or an auditor can rely on.

For years the group has weighed, sorted and processed what councils and businesses hand over: mattresses, steel, timber, tyres, white goods, e-waste, the lot. Every load crossed a weighbridge and every stream had a destination. The customer received an invoice and, at best, a diversion percentage.

YES is the reporting system built on top of that record. It takes what actually happened to the material and states it as measured tonnes by fate, recovered steel and landfill volume avoided, plus two emissions figures kept apart: the Category 5 figure for the customer's inventory, and a modelled estimate of the emissions recycling avoided. Each with the method it was calculated with.

Recycling should be more than a collection service or a percentage. It should be a measurable outcome.

Layer 01

YES Certificate

A downloadable certificate for each project or reporting period: period, streams, tonnes by fate, steel recovered, the Category 5 figure, avoided emissions reported separately, landfill avoided, method version, issuer and certificate ID.

Layer 02

YES Dashboard

A live portal that moves every time a stream is handed over, across the whole organisation, not only what passes through Recycle Group.

The principle

Only YES enters the data.

If customers entered their own figures the reports would not be credible. YES staff enter and verify every handover. A monthly email asks the customer to fill in the gaps; YES files the answers into Landfilled, Recycled or Stored.

04

Principles

Five rules every YES number is held to.

They are the reason a YES figure can go into an ESG disclosure, a Scope 3 inventory or a tender without a footnote apologising for it.

  1. Evidence over marketing.

    No "trees saved" headlines. Equivalences appear only with their source, and a figure with no source is not a figure.

  2. One method, versioned.

    Every calculation follows the YES Method. Each certificate states the version it was calculated with, so a number from last year can be reproduced.

  3. Conservative by default.

    Where two published factors exist, YES uses the more conservative one: the lower for avoided emissions, the higher for the inventory. Where no factor exists, no avoided emissions are claimed, and they are never netted against the inventory.

  4. Whole-of-organisation.

    All of a customer's recycling is reported, not only what passes through Recycle Group, and each entry says which it is.

  5. Transparent limits.

    Assumptions are labelled as assumptions. Pending categories are shown as pending. Landfilled material counts against the customer, in full.

WHY IT MATTERS

Only YES enters the data. That is why the number holds.

A report is only as credible as the weakest entry in it. Keeping data entry with YES staff, and keeping the method public and versioned, is what lets a customer sign their name under the result.
