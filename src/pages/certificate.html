07 · Method and evidenceYES Method v0.1 draft

Carbon. Steel recovered × 0.44 t CO₂-e per tonne, plus the non-steel recycled fraction × the stream's reference factor. Factors from NSW DECCW 2010, Environmental benefits of recycling, Table 4, C&I/C&D pathway. Conservative by default.

Mass. Weighbridge tonnes where available; count streams converted at the published unit weights (mattress 80 kg, tyre 9.5 kg, appliance 60 kg, vehicle 1,200 kg).

Landfill volume avoided. Uncompacted volume that would have gone to landfill, less the residual that did.

AWeighbridge docket or processor certificate. Counts in full.
BSigned count sheet or contractor report. Counts, flagged.
CCustomer statement without documentation. Recorded; excluded from the YES Score until evidenced.

Scope. Material handed over to Recycle Group under this program, plus whole-of-organisation entries evidenced by the council and graded as above. Stored material is neutral until it moves, then reclassified in the period it moves.
