05

Tender-ready

An evidenced number for the next tender.

YES data can sit inside a tender response or a contract KPI, because every figure carries its evidence grade and its method version.

  • Tender responses. Tonnes by stream and bucket, steel recovered and both emissions figures for the period, each with its YES Method reference.
  • Contract KPIs. Recovery rate and evidence share per contractor, calculated the same way for every contractor.
  • Evidence grades. Grade A, B or C on every entry, so a reader can see what a figure rests on before they rely on it.
  • Method versioning. The version is printed on the report. The figure can be reproduced later even if the factor library has moved on.
  • Exports. CSV of the ledger, the YES Report as a PDF, and a citable table for the annual report.

One method across every contractor. That is what makes the numbers comparable.

06

Questions councils ask

Straight answers.

The questions waste teams and finance teams ask first.

Do we enter the data ourselves?

No. Only YES staff enter data. If a council typed in its own figures, no report built on them would be credible to an auditor, a councillor or a resident. Your named YES data operator enters every handover against the docket, weighbridge record or count sheet, and the entry carries an evidence reference and the operator's initials.

What if material did not go through Recycle Group?

It is still reported. YES is whole-of-council: kerbside, depots, parks and any contractor. Once a month the fill-in-the-gaps email lists every stream we cannot yet account for. You tell us where it went, we enter it, and the evidence you supply decides how much it counts. A contractor report counts and is flagged. A weighbridge docket counts in full.

What is "Stored"?

Material that has been handed over but has not yet been recycled or landfilled. Coloured timber waiting for a pathway, e-waste held for a downstream processor, textiles awaiting a textile processor. Stored is neutral: it counts neither for nor against you. When it moves, the entry moves to Recycled or Landfilled and the dashboard follows.

Is the YES Score an accredited rating?

No. The YES Score is self-declared under YES's own published method, versioned with the YES Method and shown in full: material recovery rate, carbon performance and evidence quality, with the weights stated. It is proposed at v0.1. It is not an accredited rating, certification or third-party verification, and is never presented as one.

How are the emissions figures calculated?

As two figures, kept apart. The first is the Category 5 figure for your inventory, where your boundary includes the program: processing and transport, plus anything landfilled at the government's NGA landfill factor for its waste type. The second is avoided emissions, from NSW DECCW 2010 reference factors applied to recovered mass, with steel at the conservative 0.44 t CO₂-e per tonne on the recovered steel only. It is reported separately and never deducted from the first. Streams without a factor carry no estimate. Every factor, source and version is on the YES Method page.

Can we show this to residents?

Yes. The YES Report can be published as issued, and the annual report can cite its figures by method version. A community view of the dashboard, so residents can see what their hard waste became, is on the roadmap. Roadmap
