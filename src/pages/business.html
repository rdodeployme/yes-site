Where the numbers go

Ten places an evidenced environmental figure is needed.

One record of your energy, fleet, water, waste, land and community data feeds every one of them. Nothing is calculated twice and nothing is calculated differently.

  • Environmental reporting. The Yindyamarra Score and the ten category scores, with the figures behind each, by site and in total.
  • Sustainability reporting. Period-on-period movement with the method held constant.
  • ESG reporting. Figures with a source, a factor version and an evidence reference on every line.
  • Procurement reporting. What your waste, energy and fleet suppliers delivered, measured the same way for each.
  • Targets and eco standards. Every category against your target and the published eco standard, updated the month the data lands.
  • Circular-economy initiatives. Recycled-content procurement, reuse and stewardship programs, and the tonnes recovered by stream.

  • Annual sustainability reports. A citable table with the Yarta Method reference for each figure.
  • Supplier performance reporting. Evidence share and category performance per supplier, against the industry benchmark.
02

Carbon · Scope 1, 2 and 3

The emissions figure your auditor will ask about. Built line by line.

Yarta calculates Scope 1 from your fuel, Scope 2 from your electricity and the Scope 3 categories you report, including waste generated in operations (Category 5), at the National Greenhouse Accounts factors for the year. It records what an assurance provider expects to see behind each line.

What is recorded

Every month is recorded as the figures behind it: kilowatt hours and the renewable share, litres of fuel by type, kilolitres of water, tonnes by waste stream and the pathway each took. Each entry carries an evidence reference, a bill, fuel-card statement, meter read, weighbridge record or contractor report, and the grade that evidence earns.

Each figure is converted with the published factor for the year: fuel and electricity at the National Greenhouse Accounts factors, landfilled tonnes at the NGA landfill factor for the waste type. The factor edition is pinned to every line, so earlier figures stay reproducible after the library moves.

The rolling 12 months is compared with the same months of your baseline year and with your reduction target, which is how the Carbon score is set. Avoided emissions from recycling are calculated too, from published factors, and reported beside the inventory figure, never deducted from it. That is the separation the GHG Protocol asks for, and the one an assurance provider will check first.

The carbon export is the table an assurance provider expects: activity, quantity, factor, source and version, line by line, with the evidence reference beside each.

04

Multi-site

Sites, regions and the portfolio, from one record.

Every entry is tagged to a site. Roll it up by region or read the whole portfolio, with the method held constant across all of it.

A national operator does not operate in one place. A distribution centre runs a fleet and hands over pallets and film, a head office buys the electricity and refreshes its IT, a depot uses the water and clears the scrap. Yarta holds each of them as a site with its own record, and reads them together as a portfolio.

The monthly fill-in-the-gaps email goes to each site, so the person who knows where the material went is the one who answers.

  • Per site. Its own score, category tiles, monthly gap email and Yarta Report per period.
  • Per region. Sites grouped the way your organisation is run, with the same tiles at the regional level.
  • Portfolio roll-up. One Yindyamarra Score and one set of tiles for the whole organisation, built from the site records.
  • Yarta Benchmark. Compares sites against each other, against their previous period, and against the eco standards and the industry benchmark for your sector, and shows which categories moved the number.

06

Questions business asks

What you will want to know first.

The questions sustainability, procurement and finance teams ask first.

Can our existing suppliers' data be included?

Yes. Yarta reports the whole organisation, whoever supplies it. Figures from your electricity retailer, fuel-card provider, water utility, waste contractors and landscapers are entered and graded by the evidence behind them. A bill, meter read, processor certificate or weighbridge record counts in full. A contractor report counts and is flagged. A statement with no documentation is recorded and does not count until the evidence arrives.

Who enters the data?

You send the records; Yarta enters them. Your named Yarta data operator enters every figure against the bill, statement, certificate, invoice or contractor record you supply, and grades its evidence. That is what lets the figures hold up in front of an assurance provider.

What do we receive each month?

The fill-in-the-gaps email for each site, listing every category and stream we have and every one we cannot yet account for. Once your answers are entered, the dashboard is current, the score is updated and the month's figures are ready to export. A Yarta Report issues at the end of each reporting period, with the roadmap to improvement for the next one.

Can we get the underlying data?

Yes. Yarta Data exports as CSV: every entry with its date, site, category, stream, quantity, evidence grade, evidence reference and the operator's initials. The carbon export adds the scope, factor, source and method version for each line.

Who are we benchmarked against?

The eco standards first: the national targets for waste recovery, emissions and renewable electricity, and your own targets where no national standard exists. Then the published industry benchmark for your sector, drawn from public sources such as NABERS, NGER and industry reports, never from other Yarta customers. Your data is not shared with any other organisation, and no other organisation's data is shown to you.

Is this an offset?

No. Yarta reports two figures: your emissions inventory by scope, and the emissions recycling avoided, reported separately under a stated method. It does not issue offsets or credits. The avoided figure is never deducted from your inventory and should not be presented as an offset, a credit or a carbon-neutral claim.
