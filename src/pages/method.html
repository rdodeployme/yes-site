01

Principles

Six rules every YES figure obeys.

If a number on a certificate cannot be explained by one of these, it does not go on the certificate.

  1. Measured mass first

    Tonnes from a weighbridge or a processor record. Counts are converted with a stated unit weight, and the unit weight carries its own status.

  2. Recycled, Stored, Landfilled

    Every tonne lands in one of three buckets. Recycled counts for you. Landfilled counts against you. Stored is neutral until it moves.

  3. Steel is measured, not assumed

    Steel recovered is the mass that leaves as scrap, credited only on the recycled portion of a load. Until measured figures replace them, the fractions in the library are marked group or assumption.

  4. Reference factors, cited and versioned

    CO₂-e uses published factors. Each carries its source and the version of the library it entered under.

  5. Conservative by default

    Where two credible figures exist, YES uses the lower. The steel factor is the LCA net figure, not the gross.

  6. Pending means no claim

    A stream with no factor is reported as recovered tonnes with no carbon. It is marked pending, never estimated.

04

How a figure is built

From a docket to a certificate, in seven steps.

The same sequence runs for every handover. The engine does the arithmetic. Nobody types a result.

  1. Mass in

    Every handover is recorded as mass. Weighed streams arrive as tonnes from the docket. Counted streams (mattresses, tyres, white goods, vehicles) are converted with the unit weight in the library, which carries its own status.

  2. Pathway split

    The mass is split across Recycled, Stored and Landfilled. The category's default pathway applies unless the handover's actual pathway is recorded, which it is whenever the docket says so.

  3. Steel credited only on the recycled portion

    For steel-bearing streams, steel recovered is mass × steel fraction × steel recovery, capped at the recycled tonnes. A landfilled or stored load claims no steel.

  4. Carbon

    CO₂-e avoided is recovered steel × 0.44, plus non-steel recycled tonnes × the category factor. A category with no factor claims none and is marked pending on the certificate.

  5. Landfill volume avoided

    What would have gone in (mass × m³ per tonne) less what did. Mattress residual is counted at 25% of its original volume because it is shredded first.

  6. Recovery rate

    Recycled tonnes ÷ tonnes handed over. Stored tonnes are not recycled, so they do not lift the rate until they move. Landfilled tonnes pull it down.

  7. YES Score

    Recovery rate, carbon intensity and evidence share combine into one number out of 100 with the published weights below. Computed by the engine, never typed.

06

Evidence grades

Evidence is graded, not assumed.

A weighbridge docket and a customer's say-so are both recorded. They do not carry the same weight.

The YES Score's evidence-quality component is the mass-weighted share of tonnes backed by Grade A evidence. A Grade C entry can be upgraded at any time: send the docket, the operator attaches it, and the tonnes start counting.

07

Versioning and change log

The method changes only when the change is published.

Every certificate states the version it was calculated under. A later version never rewrites an earlier certificate.

VersionDateChange
v0.1 draft27 September 2026Initial factor library. NSW DECCW 2010 reference factors. Recycle Group mattress figures. Working assumptions flagged as assumptions. Pending streams carry no carbon.

Locked before the first customer certificate

  • Measured unit weights from the Recycle Group weighbridge, replacing the stated averages for mattresses, tyres, white goods and vehicles.
  • Steel recovery by stream, from processing records rather than a fraction.
  • A pathway for coloured timber, e-waste and textiles, so those streams leave Pending.
  • The TMRC per-size mattress table reconciled with the group's 30%-of-mass steel figure.

Until these are locked, the version reads "draft" on every page that uses it, and no customer certificate is issued.
