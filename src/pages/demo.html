Fill in the gaps · March

Monthly email · answered by the council · entered by YES

Once a month YES emails the council a list of every stream it cannot yet account for. The council says where each one went. YES enters it, grades the evidence, and the dashboard moves. These four items are the kind of thing a council reports that did not go through Recycle Group, so no processing or transport is attributed to them; anything landfilled still counts in the Category 5 figure at the NGA factor. Choose a bucket for each and watch the Overview.

Entries answered by email count as Grade B evidence (contractor report or signed count sheet) until a docket is supplied. Grade B counts, and is flagged on the ledger and the certificate.

    What you just saw

    Three rules that make the number hold.

    The demo lets you press the buttons. In production, a YES operator does, against a docket.

    1. Only YES enters the data

      Every handover is entered and verified by YES staff against a weighbridge docket, processor certificate or signed count sheet. Customers never type in their own figures, which is why a YES Certificate can be relied on.

    2. Every tile has a method

      Steel recovery, landfill volume, both emissions figures and the YES Score each trace to a factor with a source, a status and a version. The Category 5 inventory and the avoided emissions are never netted. Where no factor exists yet, the tonnes are counted and no avoided emissions are claimed.

    3. Stored is neutral, landfilled counts against

      Material parked at a depot does not move the score until it moves. Material sent to landfill pulls the recovery rate down. The general rubbish button shows exactly how much.
