Check a report

Is this Yarta report genuine?

Every Yarta report carries an ID made from its figures. Enter it here, or scan the code on page 1, to confirm the report is the one Yarta issued and has not been changed since.

Printed at the foot of page 1 of the report, next to the code.

What the check confirms

  1. The organisation, month and score on the report are the ones Yarta issued.
  2. No figure behind the report has changed since: the ID is made from every figure, so changing one changes the ID.
  3. Whether the month was verified by a second Yarta analyst, and when the report was issued.

A Yarta report is self-declared under the published Yarta method. It is not an accredited rating, a certification or an offset. On this site, the check covers the demo reports.
